# Residential Lease Agreement
This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Northside Properties LLC ("Owner") and the Resident(s) named below for the dwelling at 14 Maple Street, Unit 3B.
# 1. Term
The lease term begins on August 1 and ends on July 31. Rent of $1,150 is due on the first day of each month.
# 2. Security Deposit
Resident will pay a security deposit of $1,150, refundable within 30 days of move-out less any lawful deductions.
# 3. Utilities
Resident is responsible for electricity and internet. Owner pays water, sewer and trash.
# 4. Prohibition on Subletting
Resident agrees not to list or advertise the dwelling for short-term rental or occupancy by others on any website.
# 5. Violation of Lease Agreement
Any breach of the terms above is a violation of this lease agreement and may result in termination after written notice. The dwelling may be used as a private residence only.
# 6. Maintenance and Repairs
Resident must report needed repairs promptly in writing. Owner will make repairs within a reasonable time.
# 7. Signatures
Resident or Residents (all residents must sign)        Owner or Owner's Representative
